About Me

I am a fourth year student pursuing a Bachelor of Applied Science in Engineering Science at the University of Toronto, specializing in the Machine Intelligence option. My academic journey has built me a strong foundation in mathematics, physics and engineering while allowing me to dive deep into artificial intelligence and machine learning.

My passion for machine learning is driven by its transformative potential in healthcare. I aspire to develop algorithms and systems that can aid clinicians, facilitate earlier detection of disease and ultimately improve patient outcomes. I enjoy translating cutting‑edge research ideas into practical tools and collaborating with interdisciplinary teams of engineers, clinicians and researchers.

Outside the lab, I love spending time with family, going to the gym, and doing sports with friends, including basketball and snowboarding. Feel free to explore the rest of my website to learn about my research projects, publications and experiences.

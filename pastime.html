Pastimes & Interests

When I’m not immersed in research or coursework, I enjoy a variety of activities that keep me balanced and inspired:

  • Hiking & Camping: Exploring new trails and spending time in nature helps me recharge. The challenges of a good hike mirror the problem‑solving mindset needed in research.
  • Photography: I love capturing the world through a lens, particularly astrophotography. Looking up at the night sky never fails to remind me of the vastness of science and discovery.
  • Music: Whether it’s playing piano scales or listening to classical and jazz, music fuels my creativity. I occasionally experiment with generative music models just for fun.
  • Culinary adventures: Toronto’s food scene is incredibly diverse. On weekends, you’ll find me trying out new ramen shops or cooking fusion dishes at home.
  • Mentoring & Outreach: I enjoy sharing knowledge with younger students through workshops and hackathons. Helping others get started in AI/ML is hugely rewarding.

If you share similar interests or have recommendations, feel free to reach out!

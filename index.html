Welcome

Hello! I’m Kai, a fourth year Engineering Science student at the University of Toronto majoring in Machine Intelligence. My passion lies at the intersection of machine learning and healthcare, where I work on building intelligent systems to support clinicians and improve patient outcomes.

On this site you’ll find information about my research interests, publications, experiences and hobbies. Feel free to explore and get in touch!

Research Snapshot

I focus on leveraging machine learning for health. Recently I’ve explored topics such as:

  • Building foundation models for wearables to enable disease detection
  • Developing remote photoplethysmography algorithms for stand‑off heart‑rate detection to support medical triage
  • Designing 4D Gaussian splatting techniques and feature distillation for dynamic surgical scene reconstruction
  • Applying foundation models to radiological image segmentation and radiology report interpretation
  • Using computational pathology for biomarker discovery

Kai Li

kaii.li[at]mail.utoronto.ca / kail3[at]andrew.cmu.edu

I’m a 4th year student in Engineering Science at the University of Toronto. I major in Machine Intelligence, my research interests are at the intersection of machine learning, healthcare, and robotics. I work on practical systems for physiological signal analysis, medical vision, and clinical support. Currently, I am doing my undergraduate thesis at the Goldenberg Lab at UofT / Vector Institute under the guidance of Prof. Anna Goldenberg, investigating foundation models for wearables. Previously, I interned at CMU's Auton Lab under Prof. Artur Dubrawski, where I worked on heartrate algorithms for Team Chiron

Publications

Projection-based Robust and Interpretable Signal Mixing for Remote Heart Rate Estimation Outdoors

Cecilia Morales*, Chi‑En Teh*, Kai Li*, Pushpak Agrawal, Artur Dubrawski — NeurIPS Learning from Time Series for Health (TS4H) 2025 / submitted to ICRA 2026

Introduces PRISM and NATURE, the first rPPG dataset collected entirely outdoors to benchmark models under field conditions, subject to natural illumination, occlusions, and movement.

Adaptive Parameter Optimization for Robust Remote Photoplethysmography

Cecilia Morales*, Chi‑En Teh*, Kai Li*, Pushpak Agrawal, Artur Dubrawski — NeurIPS Learning from Time Series for Health (TS4H) 2025 / submitted to ICRA 2026

Introduces PRISM, a novel physics‑based rPPG method for contactless heartrate monitoring using RGB videos. Deployed in CMU's Spot Quadraped in the Darpa Triage Challenge.

FeatureEndo-4DGS: Real-Time Deformable Surgical Scene Reconstruction and Segmentation with 4D Gaussian Splatting

Kai Li*, Junhao Wang*, William Han, Ding Zhao — Machine Learning for Health (ML4H) 2025

Presents a 4D Gaussian splatting framework with feature distillation enabling real‑time rendering and semantic segmentation of dynamic surgical scenes.

ADPv2: A Hierarchical Histological Tissue Type‑Annotated Dataset for Potential Biomarker Discovery of Colorectal Disease

Zhiyuan Yang, Kai Li, Sophia Ghamoshi Ramandi, Patricia Brassard, Hakim Khellaf, Vincent Quoc‑Huy Trinh, Jennifer Zhang, Lina Cheng, Corwyn Rowsell, Sonal Varma, Kostas Plataniotis, Mahdi S. Hosseini — Journal of Pathology Informatics

Details the curation of a histological dataset of 20 000 annotated colon images to facilitate biomarker discovery in computational pathology and end-to-end training of a Vision Mamba model for histological tissue type classification.

Gastro‑Intestinal Tract Segmentation Using an Explainable 3D Unet

Kai Li and Jonathan Chan — 13th Joint Symposium on Computational Intelligence (JSCI13) 2023 / UnERD: Undergraduate Research Engineering Day @ UofT

Proposes an explainable 3D U‑Net pipeline for segmenting gastrointestinal organs in CT scans, integrating Grad‑CAM and DeepLIFT for interpretability.

Adaptive Color Mixing for Interpretable Remote Heart Rate Estimation (Abstract)

Kai Li, Pushpak Agrawal, Cecilia Morales, Chi‑En Teh, Artur Dubrawski — Medical Autonomous Care: State of the Science (MAC‑SOS) / Journal of Critical Care

Explores an adaptive colour mixing strategy for rPPG, delivering interpretable heart‑rate estimations in remote monitoring settings.
